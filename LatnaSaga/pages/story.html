The Story

From a broken guide system to a world that does not welcome outsiders.

The beginning

Han-Bin Ryu is transported to a fantasy world and receives a guide system intended to help him survive and grow stronger. The system is filled with errors, leaving him trapped in the tutorial stage for more than 20 years.

Life in Rathna

After escaping the tutorial, Han-Bin finds that the wider world is hostile toward otherworlders. He must learn how to live among people who do not automatically trust someone from another world.

What makes the story interesting?

The series combines action and fantasy with comedy, character development, and questions about prejudice and belonging.
