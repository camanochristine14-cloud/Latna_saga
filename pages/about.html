Series overview

About Latna Saga

A fantasy adventure about survival, strength, and finding a place in an unfamiliar world.

The premise

Latna Saga: Survival of a Sword King follows Han-Bin Ryu, who is transported to another world shortly after leaving military service. A malfunction in the survival guide system leaves him trapped in the tutorial stage for more than 20 years.

When he finally leaves, he discovers that otherworlders are viewed with hostility. His journey is not only about becoming stronger, but also about understanding the people and rules of this new world.

Genres

Action Fantasy Adventure Comedy

The official Tapas listing categorizes the series as Action and Fantasy.
